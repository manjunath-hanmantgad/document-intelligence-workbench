# Procurement Policy

## Approval Thresholds
All purchases above 25,000 INR require department manager approval.
All purchases above 100,000 INR require finance controller approval.
All purchases above 500,000 INR require director approval.

## Vendor Policy
Vendors must be approved before purchase orders are issued.
High-risk vendors require finance and compliance review.
Vendor contracts above 100,000 INR require legal review.

## Category Policy
Software subscriptions require security review before approval.
Hardware purchases above 50,000 INR require IT asset review.
Legal services require legal team approval.

## Duplicate Policy
Duplicate purchase requests within 30 days must be flagged and reviewed.
Repeated requests for the same vendor within 90 days require justification.

## Compliance
All purchases must have a valid business justification.
Inter-departmental transfers require department head approval.
Emergency purchases above 50,000 INR require post-facto compliance review.
