# Code Of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free community for all participants.

## Expected Behavior

1. Be respectful and constructive in discussions and reviews.
2. Focus feedback on technical outcomes and project quality.
3. Assume good intent and communicate clearly.
4. Avoid discriminatory, abusive, or disruptive behavior.

## Unacceptable Behavior

1. Harassment, intimidation, or personal attacks.
2. Discriminatory language or conduct.
3. Publishing private information without explicit permission.

## Reporting

Report issues to the project maintainer through private contact channels. Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled with discretion.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, reject contributions, or restrict participation for behavior that violates this code.
